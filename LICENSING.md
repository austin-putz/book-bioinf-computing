# Licensing

This book is licensed in two parts. Which one applies depends on whether a
file is something you **read** or something you **run**.

| | License | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** | [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](LICENSE) | The book: prose, the chapter outline, our own datasets, rendered figures, and the rendered site |
| **Code** | [MIT](LICENSE-CODE) | Everything executable: code chunks in the chapters, scripts, build tooling, and the Quarto extension and stylesheets |

**Rule of thumb:** the source that generates a figure or dataset is code
(MIT); the figure or dataset it produces is content (CC BY-NC-SA).

## What this means

- **Code:** copy it, change it, use it in your own work, including
  commercially. Keep the MIT notice.
- **Content:** read it, share it, teach from it, translate it, and adapt it,
  with credit, under the same license. You may not sell it.

## Third-party material

Data from public repositories keeps its own license and is never relicensed
under this book's terms, even when subsampled. Each dataset's source,
accession, license, and citation is listed in `data/README.md`. Figures from
papers are used only when the paper is openly licensed, and are credited in
the caption.
