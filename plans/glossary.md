## Why

Readers skip around: someone jumps to Chapter 10 without having read
Chapter 1's definition of a *node* or a *logical CPU*. A glossary gives
every term one short, plain definition in one place, and says where the
book teaches it properly. It also keeps definitions consistent: Chapter 1
and Chapter 10 must not define *scheduler* two different ways.

## What it is

- One page, `appendices/glossary.qmd` (`{#sec-glossary}`), listed under
  `appendices:` in `_quarto.yml`.
- Terms in alphabetical order. Each entry:
  - the term (and abbreviation, e.g. *GPU (graphics processing unit)*);
  - **one or two plain sentences**, the same wording a chapter uses at
    first use, written for a biology student;
  - "Taught in" links to the chapter section where the term is introduced
    (and, where useful, the chapter with the full treatment).
- No new teaching in the glossary: nothing appears there that is not in a
  chapter.

## Source of truth: `glossary.yml`

Like `misconceptions.yml`: one YAML file at the book root; the page is
generated from it, so entries stay structured and checkable.

```yaml
- id: logical-cpu
  term: "logical CPU"
  definition: >
    What the operating system counts as one processor. On many Intel and
    AMD processors each physical core appears as two logical CPUs.
  see: [cores, thread]
  chapters: [01-orientation, 35-performance]   # first = where introduced
```

- `id`: stable slug; the page anchor is `#gl-<id>`.
- `see`: related entries (shown as links).
- `chapters`: first entry is where the term is introduced.

Generated by a small executed R chunk in `glossary.qmd` (read the YAML,
write Markdown with `knitr::asis_output()`), so no extra tooling.

## Linking from chapters

- At first use in a chapter, the term is bold and defined in the text as
  now. Optionally link it: `[node](../appendices/glossary.qmd#gl-node)`.
- **[open]** Hover definitions (a tooltip on linked terms) would need a
  small Lua filter or JS in the theme; not planned for the first version.

## Rules

- Define a term in the chapter first; add it to `glossary.yml` in the same
  change. A term in the glossary with no chapter is an error.
- Same wording as the chapter (or a shortened form of it).
- Plain words; no term defined using another undefined term.
- Units and formats this book owns (FASTQ, BAM, VCF…) link to their
  format chapters.

## Open questions

- **Per book or series-wide?** Proposal: per book, with the Computing book
  owning computing and format terms; other books link to its glossary
  entries by URL rather than copying them (series `PLAN.md` §7, shared
  topics).
- **Generated from YAML or hand-written?** Proposal above is YAML; a
  hand-written page is simpler but easier to let drift.
- **Link every first use, or only bold it?** Linking helps skippers but
  adds visual noise in the early chapters.
- Place it as Appendix B (after *Cluster sites*), or as the last appendix
  before *Report an issue*?
